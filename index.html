Pre-Carve Safety Checklist

1. Clamps & Obstacles: Confirm all hold-down clamps are outside the carve boundary.
2. Bit & Zero Locked: Confirm the collet is tight and X/Y/Z zero is set on your workpiece.
3. Eye/Ear Gear & Supervision: Put on safety glasses and never leave a running spindle unattended.

Beginner's Guide: Bits, Carves & Safety

60° V-Bit (1/8"): Best all-around detail bit for crisp lettering, wildlife art, and shading (0.35–0.5mm depth).
90° V-Bit (1/8"): Wider grooves for bold signs, thick borders, and edge chamfers.
30° Engraving Bit: Needle tip for tiny text and delicate inlays (keep shallow 0.2–0.3mm).
1/8" & 1/16" Flat End Mills: Flat cutters for clearing pockets, flattening slabs, and cutting parts out.
Carve Types:
• Recessed Raster (Default): Carves dark lines into wood for natural shadow, paint-fill, or torch-burn.
• Raised Relief (Invert): Carves the background away so your design stands raised.
• Paint / Epoxy Inlay: Seal wood first, carve lines, fill with black paint/resin, then sand flat.
• 4th-Axis Rotary (A-Axis ON): Wraps Y travel around round tumblers, dowels, or rolling pins.
